# Privacy Policy for Kathas

**Effective Date:** February 16, 2026

Kathas ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

### 1. No Personal Data Collection
Kathas is designed to be a private and immersive experience. 
- We **do not** collect any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical address.
- We **do not** require you to create an account or sign in to use the app.

### 2. No Sensitive Permissions
To ensure your privacy, Kathas:
- Does **NOT** access your location.
- Does **NOT** access your camera or microphone.
- Does **NOT** access your contacts or files.
- Does **NOT** send Push Notifications.

### 3. Advertising
Kathas is **100% Ad-Free**. We do not use any advertising SDKs or share any data with advertisers.

### 4. Third-Party Analytics
We use **Google Analytics for Firebase** to help us understand how the app is being used. This information is used solely to improve the application’s performance, reading experience, and future behavior.

The data collected is **anonymous** and may include:
- Device type and model.
- Operating system version.
- App crashes or performance issues.
- Which stories (Kathas/Aartis) are most popular.

### 5. Data Security
Even though we do not collect personal data, we take the security of the anonymous app data seriously and use industry-standard measures via Google Firebase to protect it.

### 6. Children’s Privacy
Our app is safe for all ages. Since we do not collect personal information, we do not knowingly collect any data from children under the age of 13.
